# GCSE maths content coverage checklist

This author checklist follows the six content areas in the Department for
Education's [GCSE mathematics subject content and assessment objectives](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/gcse-mathematics-subject-content-and-assessment-objectives).
The publication applies to England. Use the specification for the exam board
you are taking as well, because exam boards can organise examples differently.

Each listed topic has 40 original question-bank items. The topic files are
registered in `js/gcse/index.js`. This is a topic coverage map, not a claim that
each question is a full substitute for teaching or an exam-board specification.

## Number

- [x] Place value, ordering, rounding and estimation — `place-value-rounding`, `bidmas-calculator-skills`, `estimation-error-intervals`
- [x] Four operations, factors, multiples, primes, HCF, LCM and prime factors — `bidmas-calculator-skills`, `factors-multiples-primes-hcf-lcm`, `prime-factor-form`

- [x] Powers, roots, indices, surds and standard form — `laws-of-indices`, `surds`, `standard-form`
